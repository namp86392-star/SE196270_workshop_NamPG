# REPORT ON PARTICIPATING IN AWS CLOUD AND AI DAY HANOI 2026 – WATCH PARTY IN HO CHI MINH CITY

## 1. Event Introduction

AWS Cloud and AI Day Hanoi 2026 is an event organized by Amazon Web Services (AWS), focusing on emerging technology trends such as Cloud Computing, Artificial Intelligence (AI), and modern software development.

I participated in the event through a Watch Party in Ho Chi Minh City. This was an opportunity for me to gain new knowledge, explore the AWS ecosystem, and learn about current technology trends in the IT industry.

## 2. Topics Explored

Through the event, I had the opportunity to learn about several key topics:

- **Cloud Computing:** Understanding the role of cloud technology and popular AWS services such as EC2, S3, RDS, and Lambda in application deployment and system management.
- **Artificial Intelligence & Agentic AI:** Learning about AI development trends, especially its ability to automate tasks and help people solve complex problems.
- **AI in Software Development:** Exploring how AI can support developers in writing code, debugging, and improving work efficiency.
- **Data Analytics & Cloud Security:** Understanding the importance of data analysis and information security in cloud-based systems.

## 3. Knowledge and Experience Gained

After participating in the Watch Party, I gained a better understanding of the role of Cloud Computing and AI in the development of the IT industry.

I realized that building an application requires more than just programming skills. It also involves knowledge of infrastructure, databases, security, and system deployment.

Furthermore, the rapid development of AI shows that students need to keep up with new technologies while building a strong technical foundation to use AI tools effectively.

## 4. Future Learning Goals

After the event, I plan to continue exploring AWS services, practicing application deployment on the cloud, and learning more about Generative AI and Amazon Bedrock.

In addition, I will actively participate in technology events, work on personal projects, and improve my self-learning skills to gain more practical experience.

## 5. Conclusion

AWS Cloud and AI Day Hanoi 2026 was a valuable experience that helped me expand my knowledge and gain a broader understanding of current technology trends.

Through this event, I realized the importance of combining theoretical knowledge with practical experience and continuously learning about new technologies to better prepare myself for my future career.
