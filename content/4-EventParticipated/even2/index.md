# REPORT ON PARTICIPATING IN THE FIRESIDE CHAT WITH DR. WERNER VOGELS

## 1. Event Introduction

Fireside Chat with Dr. Werner Vogels was an event featuring Dr. Werner Vogels, the Chief Technology Officer (CTO) of Amazon. The event provided an opportunity for participants to learn about technology trends, cloud computing, artificial intelligence, and experiences in building large-scale software systems.

Through this event, I had the opportunity to gain practical insights from an experienced technology expert and better understand the career development path of a software engineer.

## 2. Topics Explored

Some of the key topics discussed during the event included:

- **Cloud Computing:** Learning about the role of cloud technology and the challenges of building and operating large-scale systems.
- **Artificial Intelligence:** Understanding the impact of AI on the technology industry and how engineers can take advantage of AI in their work.
- **Software Engineering:** Recognizing the importance of building scalable, reliable, and fault-tolerant systems.
- **Innovation & Leadership:** Exploring innovative thinking, adaptability, and continuous learning in the technology industry.

## 3. Knowledge and Experience Gained

Through this event, I realized that software engineers should not focus only on learning one programming language or a specific technology. Instead, they need to develop a broad knowledge base, problem-solving skills, and an understanding of how different components of a system work together.

Furthermore, the development of AI is significantly changing the way programmers work. AI can support many tasks, but fundamental knowledge and the ability to evaluate its results remain essential.

## 4. Future Learning Goals

After the event, I realized that I need to focus more on strengthening my programming knowledge, learning about system architecture, and improving my problem-solving skills.

In addition, I will continue exploring new technologies such as Cloud Computing and AI, while gaining practical experience through personal projects.

## 5. Conclusion

Fireside Chat with Dr. Werner Vogels was a valuable experience that gave me a broader perspective on the IT industry and the expectations for future software engineers.

The most important lesson I learned is that technology knowledge must be continuously updated. However, logical thinking, the ability to learn, and problem-solving skills are the fundamental qualities that help engineers grow and succeed in the long term.
