## WEEK 6 – Learning about Amazon S3 and Data Security on AWS

### Monday – Introduction to Amazon S3

**Activities Performed:**
- Learned about the concept and role of Amazon Simple Storage Service (S3).
- Studied how Amazon S3 stores and manages data on the AWS platform.
- Explored basic concepts such as Buckets and Objects.
- Studied common use cases of Amazon S3.

**Results Achieved:**

Understood the functions of Amazon S3 and how this service supports data storage and management on AWS.

### Tuesday – Learning about Access Management in Amazon S3

**Activities Performed:**
- Learned about access control mechanisms for S3 Buckets and Objects.
- Studied the roles of Bucket Policies and IAM Policies.
- Explored Block Public Access settings.
- Studied security risks associated with improperly configured S3 access permissions.

**Results Achieved:**

Understood the access control mechanisms in Amazon S3 and recognized the importance of restricting public access to stored data.

### Wednesday – Learning about Data Protection and Encryption in S3

**Activities Performed:**
- Learned about data protection mechanisms supported by Amazon S3.
- Studied the concept of Encryption at Rest.
- Explored data encryption options available in S3.
- Studied the role of encryption in protecting data against unauthorized access.

**Results Achieved:**

Understood the importance of data encryption in Amazon S3 and the basic measures used to strengthen the security of stored data.

### Thursday – Practicing and Reviewing Amazon S3

**Activities Performed:**
- Practiced creating and managing S3 Buckets.
- Explored how to configure Static Website Hosting on Amazon S3.
- Observed how to configure access permissions for storage resources.
- Reviewed the knowledge gained about data management and security in Amazon S3.

**Results Achieved:**

Strengthened knowledge of Amazon S3, understood how to manage storage resources, and recognized important security considerations when deploying storage services on AWS.
